% -- Background and Context ----------------------------------------------------
\section{Background and Context}

Demand for video content is growing rapidly across social media and educational
platforms, yet professional-quality production remains out of reach for most
individual creators due to cost, equipment, and technical complexity. This gap
is especially pronounced for Urdu-speaking creators, where tools that understand
Urdu's linguistic patterns, phonetics, and cultural context are virtually
nonexistent.

This Final Year Project addresses that gap by building an end-to-end,
AI-powered web platform that takes a creator from a topic idea to a finished
lip-synced video in four automated stages: \textbf{AI script generation},
\textbf{text-to-speech synthesis (English and Urdu)}, \textbf{zero-shot voice
cloning}, and \textbf{lip synchronization}. No prior technical expertise is
required; a user uploads a short reference audio clip and a frontal-face video
template, provides a topic, and the platform produces a lip-synced video in
their own voice.

The system is implemented as a Next.js web application backed by a
Node.js/Express API, a Python-based AI microservices layer, and a Supabase
(PostgreSQL) database, with Clerk handling authentication and session management.

% -- Scope and Constraints -----------------------------------------------------
\section{Scope and Constraints}

\textbf{In Scope}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Web application (Next.js 14, TypeScript) with Clerk authentication,
        protected dashboard, and user management
  \item AI script generation and iterative refinement in English and Urdu using
        LLMs via OpenRouter (Claude, GPT-4, Gemini), with version history and
        draft saving
  \item Six-stage content creation workflow: language selection $\to$ script
        method $\to$ script generation/refinement $\to$ script review $\to$ TTS
        synthesis $\to$ lip synchronization
  \item English TTS via a Coqui\,TTS FastAPI microservice
  \item Urdu TTS via a two-stage pipeline: Parler-TTS (Indic) for base phonetic
        synthesis, followed by OpenVoice\,V2 zero-shot tone-color transfer
  \item Lip synchronization using Wav2Lip on user-uploaded frontal-face video
        templates
  \item Node.js/Express backend with Supabase (PostgreSQL), Clerk JWT-secured
        API, and RLS-enforced data isolation
  \item Responsive dark-theme UI with real-time progress tracking for
        long-running AI jobs
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Out of Scope}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Mobile applications (iOS or Android)
  \item Real-time or streaming video generation
  \item Multi-speaker or dialogue-based videos
  \item Languages other than English and Urdu
  \item Per-user custom model training (zero-shot approach eliminates this)
  \item Post-production video editing beyond lip synchronization
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Constraints}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Three-person development team with a fixed FYP academic deadline
  \item GPU inference for Wav2Lip and TTS models limited to local and free-tier
        compute; processing latency will vary by hardware availability
  \item Urdu output quality bounded by the current capability of the open-source
        Parler-TTS Indic model
  \item No allocated budget for paid cloud GPU instances; all third-party
        services use free or academic tiers
\end{itemize}

% -- Risks and Assumptions -----------------------------------------------------
\section{Risks and Assumptions}

\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.8cm}
                >{\centering\arraybackslash}p{2.0cm}
                >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{6.8cm}}
  \toprule
  \thead{Risk / Assumption} & \thead{Type} & \thead{Mitigation / Note} \\
  \midrule
  High inference latency on limited GPU
    & Risk
    & Jobs processed asynchronously with status polling; users notified on completion \\
  \talt
  Urdu TTS naturalness bounded by Parler-TTS Indic model
    & Risk
    & MOS evaluations conducted; OpenVoice\,V2 tone transfer mitigates
      perceptual flatness \\
  OpenRouter API rate limits or key expiry
    & Risk
    & Automatic retry logic and multi-model fallback (Claude $\to$ GPT-4 $\to$
      Gemini) implemented \\
  \talt
  Wav2Lip quality degrades on non-frontal or low-resolution video
    & Risk
    & UI enforces frontal-face upload requirement with guidance notes \\
  LLM produces unnatural or overly formal Urdu scripts
    & Risk
    & Custom language-specific system prompts with conversational Urdu guidance
      developed and iterated \\
  \talt
  Zero-shot cloning requires $\geq$15\,s of clean reference audio
    & Assumption
    & Minimum duration enforced in UI; quality feedback shown if audio is too
      short or noisy \\
  All four phases completable within the FYP deadline
    & Assumption
    & Phases 1\,\&\,2 complete; Phases 3--4 time-boxed with explicitly scoped
      deliverables to prevent scope creep \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
